## Iteration and retest

Initial inspection caught the asset-library display-grid offset, ill-fitting
garments, scalp surface overlap, beard depth in profile, fingers/cuffs not aligned
and an ink hull blocking studio lights. Corrected relative scaffold transforms,
snapshotted normals before shell offsets, fitted beard/brow/moustache to anatomy,
kept covered trouser geometry out of outer layers, fitted cuffs/boots/mantle and
disabled non-camera hull visibility. Rebuilt and inspected all views again.

The first automated clipping assertion incorrectly treated the intentional face
bust crop as a full-body canvas. Revised that check to require the head top while
allowing the deliberately cropped shoulders. All final checks passed afterwards.
The final verifier also avoids Blender's deprecated material `use_nodes` getter.
