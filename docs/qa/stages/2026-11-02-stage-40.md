## Implementation and review

Preserved stage39 byte-for-byte. Diagnosed its collar, cloth hems, boot profiles
and shoulder arcs in colour and neutral geometry. Reviewed a warden pilot before
applying the geometry method to the mercenary and wayfarer.

Curved monotone profiles replace linear radius transitions; radial samples remove
visible chords. One cage subdivision level refines head/cloth/shoulder forms before
shell thickness. Small glove/hairline relax, rounded leather corners, more bevel
segments and a sewn neckline finish soften the selected edges. Steel boundaries
remain defined. Broader cape folds and compressed reference-light contrast reduce
false angular paint patches. A uniform wool pass was rejected for losing the
painted surface. Rejected renders/source identity are in `review-history/`.

The same3 original design references, palette, anatomy scaffold, pose and camera
are retained. No new AI generation, paid job, installation, live GUI brush strokes,
visible app launch or desktop input. No player saves or combat code changed.
The builder adds in-memory refinements to the pinned original factory; the original
source and its101-file delivery remain intact. Projection bounds caching removes
repeated mesh scans in the denser-source build without changing its mapping math.
