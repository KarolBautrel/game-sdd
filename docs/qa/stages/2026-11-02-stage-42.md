chest/upper fauld, trousers through boot cuffs and thigh fabric through skirt.
Body-surface ray probes distinguished these from shading. A per-vertex radial
armor fit produced uneven surfaces and was rejected; the final armor uses smooth
broader construction profiles. Cloth intentionally hidden beneath rigid gorget/
shoulder crowns is masked. Skirt and lower trousers are fitted with clearance.

the under-armor garment mask now cover those areas. Cloak top follows the actual
back-armor curve and has attachment studs rather than hovering as a flat plane.

and the actual vamp-derived ankle band resolved them. Closest-surface toe-seam
placement picked inner shell faces; outward ray projection keeps the seam on the
exterior. Two coplanar seam errors were repaired without relaxing audit thresholds.
