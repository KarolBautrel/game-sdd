## Iteration and visual assessment

Actual render inspection caught brightness-key damage to skin/steel, backdrop
paint on boots/arm gaps, profile occlusion, hair ridges, garment intersections,
jagged neck/collar transitions and foreign belt/trouser paint on the cape.
Replaced shader colour-keying with connected-backdrop mattes/paint bleed and
semantic UV fitting in Aseprite. Rebuilt the fitted core, open neckline and lower
neck fit; reduced inflated/hair-tube geometry; used separate cloak paint and
kept the drape outside the torso. Covered trouser geometry was conformed inside
the coat after a small breakthrough was visible in the actual FBX reimport.

Preserved rejected actual renders in study `review-history/`. A temporary collar
and overaggressive lower-neck deformation were rejected during the loop; final
sources were rebuilt. One overlapping retry process was stopped; final builds
ran sequentially and final checks use the completed files. The first fresh-scene
test failed because its empty scene had no World; the harness now creates that
World before calling the retained shared studio setup. This was a harness defect,
not a missing exported model dependency.

Observed comparison: adult silhouette/face and fitted layered costume read closer
to the selected painted direction than the old rounded A. Surface/material detail
is substantially carried by painted texture. Neutral geometry reviews remain
available to show the actual construction; the models are still fairly simple.
Technical PASS does not establish professional art quality or the user's approval.
