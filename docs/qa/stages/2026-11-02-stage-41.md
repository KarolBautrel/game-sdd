## Corrections and limits

- First official download mirror stalled; the second official mirror completed.
  Archive checksums are pinned in setup and metadata; no third-party asset mirror.
- The first eye shader omitted the source's cornea-mask alpha and produced black
  openings. The alpha link is restored and asserted in source and imported FBX.
- Original lower neck/trapezius protruded outside the collar, including a smaller
  profile patch. Fitted it inside the garment and rechecked the profile.
- Mesh audit rejected skin/hair coplanarity and two overlapping card faces. Local
  offsets/targeted card repair resolved all errors; no tolerance was relaxed.
- Spherical shoulder fillers were tried and rejected because they covered the
  steel with oversized plain shapes. The retained costume is used instead.
- The render-background assertion initially sampled a deliberately cropped bust
  shoulder at the bottom corner. It now checks the actual upper background;
  full-body clipping checks remain active. The corrected verifier passed.
- Existing costume has a detached fragment1.1% of its faces,10.2mm from the rest.
  Head/eye/hair/brow assembly is11.4mm from the nearest costume surface. The neck
  is visually inside the collar; the static assembly is not a continuous body or
  rigging proof. These five warnings remain explicit, not a watertight claim.
- Hair remains a legacy alpha-card hairstyle; facial skin is deliberately modest
  in microdetail. Earlier costume paint/baked light and some open plate/sleeve
  overlaps remain. This pilot solves the skin mapping method, not every costume
  surface or articulation detail. FBX retains image maps but not every Blender
  skin-shader parameter; compare its actual imported views.
- No independent visual reviewer or physical game session was used. Do not infer
  user approval from an audit or the assertion count. No commit/push requested.
