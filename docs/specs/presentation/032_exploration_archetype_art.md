## Shared visual system

- Approved mine reference: painted ashen parchment, soot leather, blackened iron,
  muted crimson, ivory type and restrained ember light. Bloodlines is high-level
  inspiration; no1:1asset/component copies.
- Keep layout, frame language, typography, room labels, portrait/HP treatment and
  current/selected/locked distinctions consistent. Main map~70%, inspector~26%,
  compact supplies/company. Two approved hero identities and matching red HP.
- Change environment through chart surface, room artwork, palette accents,
  inspector vignette and small original identity seal. Do not redesign the whole
  interface per biome. Sample portrait/text pixels remain concept placeholders.

## Sample and production boundary

Reuse the approved mine unchanged and generate three full-screen samples against
that same reference. Save exact prompts, provenance, dimensions/hashes, approval
status and a local gallery that opens each original. These are design previews.

With the family approved, separate shared frames/fonts/native controls
from archetype substrates, transparent room vignettes, environment props and seals.
Never ship a concept as the whole interface. Labels, paths, selection and costs
stay native/data-driven; background art cannot bake false graph connections into
generated maps. Existing domains still own guards, event focus, costs and saves.
