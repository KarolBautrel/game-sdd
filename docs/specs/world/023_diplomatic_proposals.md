Stage97c,2026-11-10. Follows the automated supply-pipeline gate. Extends
world017/019; proof [slice024](slices/024_diplomatic_proposals.md).

Each own relation observation reports the kinds of live pending bilateral pact
offers and whether a live ceasefire offer exists, in either direction. Include
only this actor's pair, only unexpired pending records, and no foreign private
intentions. These are known negotiations, not signed agreements.

New classic proposals and model capabilities do not propose a kind already
active or pending for that pair. In particular, replying to an incoming pact or
peace offer must not also propose its duplicate from the same old observation.
Replying preserves the original identical terms and counterparty consent.
Expired incoming trade/pact/peace records are not offered as fresh responses.
An expired pending peace record must not block a new lawful ceasefire proposal;
normal archival and retained receipts still own the old record's lifetime.
The existing archive phase marks overdue pending peace offers expired, just as
for trade offers, before its ordinary closed-record retention logic. Reading or
loading alone never performs that mutation.

threshold or suppression of actual operator failures. Explicit duplicate,
late/stale or conflicting commands still reject at the same authoritative gate.
Two actors proposing from simultaneous snapshots can still contend at execution;
delayed model responses can become obsolete. Record these actual outcomes.
Expired, accepted and rejected offers remain subject to the existing archive /
receipt rules; do not erase canonical obligations to clean a diagnostic counter.

requests/capability hashes and saved negotiations remain loadable unchanged.
