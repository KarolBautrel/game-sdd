## Role, clock and autonomy

The mobile mercenary company participates in a world progressing on its own game
clock without requiring company input, visits, contracts or success. Factions,
towns, rivals and threats have independent goals/means. Dungeon crawling and
tactical encounters remain the company's main interventions; Ravenford is an
opening hub, not a mandatory permanent base. Preserve approved art/navigation/UI.

Autonomy does not authorize closed-profile wall-clock catch-up, background
services, new menu/pause time, paid inference or desktop interaction. Existing
travel/rest/managed-expedition time authorities remain.

## Actors and simple strategic means

Reuse the four existing powers initially. Each has one main ambition and one
secondary concern. Add only actors with implemented behaviour/content: patrols,
rivals, bandits, monsters/cults and civilians. Persist identity, source, location
or coarse regional task, available strength/means and current commitment.

World decisions need a small assessment of means, military readiness, stability
and supply condition. Town profiles/doctrine explain variation. Additional
profession/recipe/tax/commodity detail is not a prerequisite. Existing stocks can
support these assessments internally; later replacement with abstract budgets
requires explicit schema/version and payment/conservation semantics first.
Exact company inventory, money, provisions, rewards and tactical results retain
their authorities. Qualitative labels cannot mint spendable resources.

## Episodes own change

Persist cause, actor/target references, phase, commitments, progress, resolution
and aftermath. Intended phases: pressure, warning, commitment, action, resolution,
aftermath, with supported branches. Jobs, reports, danger/control and encounters
derive from those same facts. Text/report receipt cannot accomplish an outcome.
First families: threatened roads, border disputes, dangerous sites and recovery.
Economics is one possible pressure, not the cause of every episode.

NPC actions can advance, fail or resolve episodes without company commands.
Actual means/strength are committed/spent/recovered; replacements have supported
sources and time. The first slice includes a real supported camp/encounter and
complete response, not an unimplemented interactive marker.

## Politics, company choices and persistent places

Campaigns pursue reachable objectives: passage, protection, an outpost or a
specific threat. Include warnings, preparation, travel, bounded rules-based
clashes, supported control changes and settlement/recovery. Models cannot assign
winners, create armies or sign another faction's terms. Occupation, attrition and
competing priorities limit conquest; no hidden level scaling or compulsory war.

Contracts follow active causes and reserve payable rewards. Player/NPC actions
use explicit claims/terms where needed. Races, deadlines, exclusivity and alternate
completion are disclosed before acceptance. Preserve earned payment/receipts and
resolve each episode once. Do not retroactively change existing contracts.

Current Old Mine restoration stays permanent. Later occupation elsewhere requires
a new arriving actor/cause and episode ID, preserving earlier history. Layouts
remain stable within an episode. No daily reset, duplicate loot or entry-triggered
reroll. Limited NPC reach/competence and resulting consequences leave changing
useful opportunities without reserving the entire world for the company.

## Global detail and knowledge

The first slice uses the real existing region. Six towns/nine bound sites are not
already a complete province system. Later connected provinces use coarse daily
progression away from the company and terrain-safe parties where relevant.
Transfers retain identity, elapsed travel, obligations and once-only resolutions;
region entry must not reroll history or duplicate abstract forces.

Public confirmed facts, dated sourced rumours, company observations and private
faction intentions remain distinct. Relevant news arrives through supported
settlements/travellers/scouts. Map/inspector/faction/chronicle should explain what
changed, where, who acted and the resulting company choices. No new global
dashboard or omniscient narrative is needed for the initial slice.

## Classic first and compatibility

A complete classic planner uses the same bounded world/episode actions as a
later model strategist. A live provider is not a prerequisite for99..103.
Recorded examples stay labelled authored/offline. Hosting/model/cost choices
remain unresolved and are deferred until local autonomous world proof.

Preserve current97b/c work, profiles, terrain/art and accepted obligations.
New episode/actor/control registries need versioned ownership, schemas and
executable slices. Do not insert new IDs into legacy fixed-atlas fields or
rewrite immutable geography. No default-campaign promotion in this design.
