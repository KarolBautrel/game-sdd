## What is in the game

One original hooded raider: short cowl, weathered leather, asymmetrical shoulder,
burgundy sash, boots/gloves and a cleaver in the right hand. It appears in the
new selectable **Ash-road raiders** encounter and as the melee enemy in the
existing roadside ambush. Combat stats, loadouts, AI and rewards are unchanged.
It has eight actual camera directions rather than mirrored equipment, idle,
walk, attack, hit and death; final death pose is held after resolver death.

The visible mesh/UVs/cleaver were authored in Blender; Quaternius CC0 supplies the
65-bone compatible skeleton and five motion clips. The original reference was
generated by the built-in image tool and also provides the material atlas.
No paid cloud generator, account login or external generation credits were used.
Cloth, limbs and plates use deliberately simple geometry for the tactical zoom;
this is not a sculpted close-up hero or a claim of anatomical perfection.

## Sources and repeatable build

- assets_src/characters/ash_raider/reference.png and PROMPT.txt: exact generated
  target and prompt; target selected by the agent, user approval absent.
- ash_raider.blend, ash_raider.fbx, textures/ash_raider_albedo.png: editable mesh,
  skin, five clips and portable atlas. The FBX was re-imported and checked for
  mesh/UV/weights/texture/bone movement in every clip.
- assets_src/characters/ash_raider/manifest.json: file identities and provenance.
- tools/blender/build_ash_raider.py: original geometry/UV authoring, fitted rig,
  pose setup and render recipe; --preview creates front/side/rear/game/action
  previews. Defaults render256 raw frames in artifacts/character-pilot/raw.
- tools/aseprite/ash_raider.lua: alpha threshold, warm dark contour and8-step RGB
  cluster quantization; exports256 PNGs and40 editable .aseprite action/view files.
  Every exported frame has transparent margins and a fixed ground pivot.
- tools/blender/verify_ash_raider.py: actual FBX re-import with clip-motion checks.
- resources/licenses/quaternius-character-motion.txt: shipped motion attribution.

Rebuilds must regenerate the provenance hashes after export and rerun the checks.
The motion source is the pinned downloaded UAL1 Standard dependency in TOOLING.md.
Godot ignores the character source directory; the runtime imports only exported
sprites. Never point Godot's importer at the working .blend as a workaround.

Render recipe: orthographic width4.2m, elevation27 degrees,448x352 RGBA, pivot
(224,266), neutral fixed studio, CPU Cycles4 samples, Khronos PBR Neutral. The
same framing is used across directions/actions. During death renders, horizontal
pelvis translation is recentered so the falling body stays by the original tile;
the delivered FBX retains the source animation. Preview cameras use different
framing and are labelled construction views, not matched pixel comparisons.

Runtime renders the fixed canvas at native size in an overflowing TextureRect,
positioned(-32,-26) relative to the legacy384x240 unit node. Its ground anchor is
unchanged. Extending only the canvas prevents weapons/fallen bodies being cropped
without resizing or moving the character per frame. Legacy sprites keep their
existing paths, scaling and timing.
