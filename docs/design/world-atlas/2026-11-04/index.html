The Crownlands ? world and provinces

Actual Godot GPU captures, not concept mockups. Four provinces, twenty connected destinations, explicit per-leg journeys. Illustrations follow the accepted Marches family; the original Marches plate is unchanged.

The fixture uses a separate memory-only company with 30 food / 200 gold. It browses provinces, previews/cancels a route and travels through six adjacent legs to Black Keep, stopping for a real road event, then returns to Ravenford using exactly the retained 3-food reserve. Native keyboard/pointer events; no desktop input or player save. Physical player review is pending.

Run from the game repository: & .\tools\qa\start_game.ps1 -Scene app, then choose World atlas. New keep/abbey/camp sites are travel landmarks; interiors/services remain future content.
