powers plus supported patrols/rivals/bandits/threats/civilians with goals and
limited means. Persistent episodes own pressure/warning/commitment/action /
resolution/aftermath. NPCs can resolve or fail situations without company visits.
Jobs, danger/control and news derive from actual episode facts. Economics is
modest support, not the main product. Exact company ownership/payments and
existing obligations keep their authorities.

collapse of stocks into labels or new save migration. Old Mine stays restored
where already completed. Player/NPC races must respect published accepted terms;
new occupations/rewards need new real causes, not daily resets. Current region
is not advertised as a completed province simulation. World time remains the
game clock with existing pauses, not an offline service.
