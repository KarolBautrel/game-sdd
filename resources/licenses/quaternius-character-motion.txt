Ash-road Raider uses the compatible humanoid skeleton and these motion sources:
Sword_Idle, Walk_Loop, Sword_Attack, Hit_Chest, Death01.
The visible raider mesh, UV layout and cleaver were authored for this project.
Its reference/albedo atlas was generated with the built-in image generation tool.
The downloaded mannequin mesh is not part of the visible raider or game export.

The complete original tactical roster also reuses the compatible65-bone rig and
Idle_Loop, Walk_Loop, Sword_Attack, Hit_Chest and Death01 for humanoids. Visible
meshes are project-authored. Bow/crossbow/spear handling, grounded spear death,
wraith collapse and all nonhumanoid creature rigs/actions are authored for this
project. Shared material-atlas provenance is retained under
assets_src/characters/roster/. No Quaternius visible mannequin mesh is shipped.
