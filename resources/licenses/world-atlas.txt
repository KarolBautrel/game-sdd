WORLD ATLAS ART PROVENANCE

The four Crownlands world/province illustrations were generated for this project
using the connected built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on 2026-11-04, with
the project's existing approved Marches illustration as a style reference.
They were imported and exported through Aseprite. These are not downloaded
Kenney, Shikashi or other third-party map-pack assets.
